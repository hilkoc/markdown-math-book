# Introduction {-}

This is a sample book. It shows every feature of the pipeline in two short
chapters. Replace these files with your own chapters.

An unnumbered chapter, such as this one, is marked with `{-}` after its
heading. Pandoc gives it no number, and theorems in later chapters are
numbered as if it were not there. Use it for a preface, a foreword or an
afterword.

Chapter [1](#pythagoras) finds all Pythagorean triples. Chapter [2](#cubics)
shows why the same method fails for cubic curves.
